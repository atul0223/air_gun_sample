Before you buy.

Do I need an arms licence?

Under the Arms Rules 2016, .177 (4.5 mm) air guns under 20 joules do not need a licence. Higher-powered models can. Ask us about any specific model.

How old do I have to be?

18 or older, with Aadhaar or another government ID.

PCP or CO2?

PCP rifles run on air filled to high pressure with a pump or tank, and give more power and consistency. CO2 runs on small 12 g cartridges, which suits pistols and casual shooting.

Do you ship outside Meerut?

Yes, across India. Message us your pin code for a delivery estimate.

Can I visit the shop?

Yes. We are in Ganganagar, near IIMT University. Call before you come so the piece you want is ready.
